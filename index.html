Montana Elderly Renter Credit — Property Check

Does your rent qualify?

To claim Montana's Elderly Renter Credit, the property you rent must be subject to Montana property tax. Enter the address below to check.

Look up an address

We search Montana's own public Cadastral parcel records — the same data the state uses.

How this works

  1. You enter the rental property's address.
  2. We query the Montana State Library / Department of Revenue's public Cadastral parcel database for a matching parcel.
  3. We show you how that parcel is coded — taxable or tax-exempt — and link straight to the state's official Property Record Card so you (or your tax preparer) can confirm before filing.
This tool is a research aid, not tax advice. Property records occasionally lag ownership or exemption changes, and some properties (like certain government, tribal, or nonprofit-owned buildings) are only partly exempt. Before filing, confirm the property's status on the linked official Property Record Card, or contact your county Department of Revenue office or a tax professional.
